Guardian+ Documentation

Everything you need to know about setting up and using the Guardian+ app.

01. Introduction

Guardian+ ek advanced personal safety application hai jo users ko emergency situation mein immediate help provide karne ke liye design kiya gaya hai. Yeh app background services aur sensors ka use karke aapki safety ensure karta hai.

02. Installation & Setup

  • Step 1: Website se APK download karein aur install karein.
  • Step 2: App open karke allow all permissions
  • Step 3: Allow display over other apps, so that touch can work.
  • Step 4: Battery optimization - select do not optimize.
  • Step 5:Fill the deatils in profile section.
Note: Android 6.0+ devices par "Battery Optimization" ko disable karna zaroori hai taaki background services sahi se kaam karein.

03. Core Features

  • when you on the guardian mode after all permission setup , there is blue dot get visible in your screen tap that 5 times :to share Real-time GPS coordinates with battery status to your contacts.
  • Shake you mobile 2 3 times call will redirect: Emergency mein phone shake karne par automatically preset number par call lag jati hai.
  • set your on word for recording and then whenever you say that word recording get start, and for off the recording , turn off the guardain mode: Situation detect hote hi app background mein audio record karna shuru kar deta hai.

04. Privacy & Permissions

Humari priority aapka data secure rakhna hai. Guardian+ wahi data access karta hai jo emergency alerts ke liye zaroori ho:

  • Location: Emergency alert mein position batane ke liye.
  • SMS: Bina internet ke contacts ko message bhejne ke liye.
  • Accessibility: Background triggers ko monitor karne ke liye.
